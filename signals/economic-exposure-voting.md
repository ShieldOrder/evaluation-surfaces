# Economic Exposure Voting

Status: signal definition  
Scope: preference and sentiment signaling  
Posture: descriptive, non-prescriptive

## Definition

Economic exposure voting is a legitimacy signal that weights input according to
economic exposure to an asset or system.

It is commonly implemented as stake-weighted or coin-weighted voting, but the
core property is exposure alignment, not the mechanism itself.

This signal expresses how actors with capital at risk prefer a decision to resolve.

## What this signal measures well

Economic exposure voting is well suited to measure:

- alignment of preferences among economically exposed participants
- aggregate sentiment weighted by capital at risk
- directional support or opposition to outcomes affecting asset value
- tolerance for tradeoffs that primarily affect economic outcomes

This signal is strongest when the decision surface is preference-oriented.

## What this signal does not measure

Economic exposure voting does not measure:

- technical correctness or safety
- security or cryptographic soundness
- feasibility of implementation
- execution quality or delivery capability
- accountability for failure
- compliance with non-economic constraints

Weighting input by exposure does not create technical authority.

## Surface fitness

Best-fit decision surfaces:

- preference and sentiment signaling

Weak or invalid fit:

- protocol correctness
- execution and accountability
- resource allocation requiring fiduciary evaluation

Using this signal outside its fitness domain creates category errors.

## Common misapplications

Failure modes occur when economic exposure voting is treated as:

- binding authority over correctness decisions
- a substitute for expert review
- an enforcement mechanism
- proof of feasibility
- a justification for bypassing verification

These uses inflate preference into authority.

## Relationship to other signals

Economic exposure voting often coexists with:

- expert review
- committee evaluation
- open discussion

Conflicts between signals are expected.
This signal does not resolve such conflicts.

## Interpretation constraint

Economic exposure voting expresses preference, not correctness.

Its legitimacy is conditional on being applied only to decision surfaces
where preference is the relevant dimension.
