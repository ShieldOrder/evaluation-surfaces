# Open Discussion

Status: signal definition  
Scope: qualitative input and narrative formation  
Posture: descriptive, non-prescriptive

## Definition

Open discussion is a legitimacy signal produced through public, unstructured
deliberation among participants.

It includes forum threads, mailing lists, chat platforms, social media, and
other open communication channels.

The signal derives from visibility and participation, not authority,
expertise, or economic exposure.

## What this signal measures well

Open discussion is well suited to measure:

- qualitative concerns and objections
- value judgments and tradeoffs
- narrative framing and community norms
- early detection of overlooked risks
- interpretability of proposals

This signal is strongest for surfacing issues, not resolving them.

## What this signal does not measure

Open discussion does not measure:

- technical correctness or safety
- comparative prioritization
- economic exposure alignment
- delivery capability
- binding consensus

Volume or intensity of discussion is not evidence of correctness.

## Surface fitness

Best-fit decision surfaces:

- preference and sentiment signaling
- exploratory framing and problem discovery

Weak or invalid fit:

- protocol correctness
- resource allocation
- execution enforcement

Treating discussion outcomes as binding authority is commonly associated with category errors.

## Failure modes

Common failure modes include:

- loudness bias
- participant self-selection
- narrative capture
- topic drift
- false consensus inference

Visibility does not imply representativeness.

## Relationship to other signals

Open discussion often precedes or informs:

- expert review
- committee evaluation
- preference signaling

It should not substitute for them.

## Interpretation constraint

Open discussion functions as advisory input.

It informs judgment but does not bind outcomes.
