# Scope

This repository defines a taxonomy for evaluating governance decisions by separating:

- decision surfaces (what is being decided and under what constraints)
- signals (what is being observed and what it can credibly evaluate)
- failure modes (how signals get misapplied and what breaks as a result)

This work is descriptive and analytical. It does not prescribe reforms, mechanisms, or outcomes.

In scope:
- terminology for decision surfaces and signals
- analysis of common failure modes in governance evaluation
- neutral language that reduces category errors in discussion

Out of scope:
- recommending governance mechanisms
- endorsing specific institutions or decisions
- defining scoring, ranking, or enforcement systems
- adjudicating disputes about legitimacy or authority

Posture:
- descriptive, non-prescriptive
- mechanism neutral
- artifact-first, citation friendly where possible
