# CONTRIBUTING

This repository is analytical infrastructure.

It documents decision surfaces, legitimacy signals, and governance failure modes as descriptive taxonomies. It does not advocate mechanisms, propose reforms, or recommend governance changes.

## Acceptable contributions

Contributions may include:
- Clarifications to definitions
- Corrections of category errors
- Identification of missing decision surfaces or signal types
- Additional failure mode diagnostics
- Improvements to internal consistency or precision of language

All contributions must remain:
- Descriptive, not prescriptive
- Mechanism-neutral
- Free of policy advocacy
- Free of recommendations or calls to action

## Out of scope

The following will not be accepted:
- Proposals for new governance mechanisms
- Arguments for or against specific voting systems
- Moral, ideological, or political framing
- Protocol change suggestions
- References to specific ZIPs, upgrades, or pending decisions

## Review posture

Submissions are evaluated solely on analytical clarity, correctness, and consistency with the repository’s descriptive scope.

This is infrastructure for reasoning, not a venue for debate.
