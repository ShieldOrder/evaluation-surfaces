# Posture

This repository is analytical infrastructure.

It is not a proposal. It is not an advocacy effort. It is not a governance mechanism.

It exists to reduce category errors where a signal is applied to a decision type it cannot credibly evaluate.

Non-authority:
- no mandates
- no enforcement
- no claim of representation
- no claim of consensus beyond internal coherence

Interpretation:
- these documents provide one taxonomy among many possible taxonomies
- disagreement is expected
- communities and institutions retain full discretion to interpret or ignore this work
