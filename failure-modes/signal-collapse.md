# Signal Collapse

Status: failure mode definition  
Scope: misapplication of legitimacy signals  
Posture: descriptive, non-prescriptive

## Definition

Signal collapse occurs when a legitimacy signal is treated as authoritative
for a decision surface it cannot credibly evaluate.

This is a category error between:
- what kind of decision is being made
- what the signal is capable of measuring

Signal collapse is not a disagreement. It is a structural mismatch.

## Core pattern

1. A decision surface has specific correctness or accountability requirements
2. A signal is applied that does not measure those requirements
3. The outcome is justified by the signal’s existence rather than its fitness

The error is procedural, not ideological.

## Common collapse forms

### Authority inflation

A non-binding signal (e.g. preference or discussion) is treated as binding
authority.

Legitimacy is inferred from participation rather than decision fitness.

### Correctness bypass

Signals expressing preference override safety, feasibility, or correctness
constraints.

The decision is framed as “decided” despite unresolved technical failure modes.

### Accountability dilution

Responsibility for outcomes becomes non-attributable.

When failure occurs, no actor can be held accountable because authority was
diffused across an unfit signal.

### Legitimacy laundering

The process itself is cited as legitimacy:
- “We voted”
- “The community decided”
- “There was consensus”

The signal replaces evaluation rather than informing it.

### Goodhart effects

Actors optimize for the signal rather than the underlying objective.

The measurement becomes the target.

## Why signal collapse persists

Signal collapse is attractive because it:
- simplifies complex decisions
- converts judgment into procedure
- shields actors from accountability
- resolves disputes socially rather than analytically

These incentives are structural, not malicious.

## Detection heuristic

Signal collapse is likely when:

- the signal cannot explain *why* the decision is correct
- dissent is dismissed by reference to process alone
- accountability for failure is unclear or distributed
- signal choice becomes the core of the dispute

## Relationship to this repository

This repository exists to make signal collapse visible.

By explicitly separating:
- decision surfaces
- legitimacy signals
- failure modes

Category errors become diagnosable rather than rhetorical.
